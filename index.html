Wake Up Tracker — Privacy Policy

Effective August 6, 2026

The short version

Wake Up Tracker collects no data. Nothing you do in the app leaves your device.

What the app accesses, and why

  • Health (read-only): with your permission, the app reads your step counts and recorded sleep from Apple Health. It uses them to cross-check when you were really awake. It never writes to Health, and never changes anything there.
  • Activity you log: app-open events you choose to log (for example via Shortcuts automations) are stored on your device so the app can infer your real wake and sleep times.

All of this stays in the app's local storage on your iPhone. There is no server, no account, and no sync.

What we don't do

  • No user accounts or sign-in.
  • No analytics or tracking.
  • No advertising tools.
  • The app makes no network requests, and your data is never transmitted.

Beta testing (TestFlight)

If you use the app through TestFlight, Apple may share crash logs and basic usage statistics with the developer under Apple's own privacy terms. The app itself still collects and transmits nothing.
